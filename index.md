    <p align="left">Victoria Brodie is a technical writer and a documentation strategist who specializes in cybersecurity. She currently works at <a href="https://arcticwolf.com/">Arctic Wolf</a> , where she is leading migration and rebranding efforts following the acquisition of <a href="https://www.sevcosecurity.com/">Sevco Security</a>, where she spent almost four years developing a documentation program from scratch.</p>
    <p align="left">Victoria believes that documentation is a product function and should never be an afterthought. Her work encompasses a combination of information architecture, API documentation, and in-app content strategy. Victoria has worked on building documentation from scratch in some particularly high-stakes areas: elections management software distributed across 18 states, event marketing platforms, and cybersecurity solutions utilized by security teams operating in the increasingly complicated environment. Victoria has also delivered talks at <a href="https://bsidesbloomington.org/schedule-2023/f/are-vulnerability-scanners-dead-transcending-cves">BSides events</a> about the obsolescence of vulnerability scanners, giving a quick historical overview of keyboard instruments as proof that no technology disappears but rather becomes an integral part of something else.</p>
